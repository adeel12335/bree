Author Press Release

Hannah Walker Mystery · Book 2

Deadly VowsEchoes of College Crimes

“What begins with love... ends with silence.”

International Impact Book Awards winner (April 2026)

Shop Now

AmazonApple BooksBarnes & NobleSmashwords

Hannah Walker swore she left the darkness of college behind. But when she returns to her hometown for her best friend Connie’s wedding, an eerie sense of déjà vu creeps in. Whispers from the past echo louder, and the nightmare begins anew.

Connie is found dead mere hours before her vows.

Beside her body lies a chilling riddle—eerily similar to those Hannah once unraveled in a string of college killings. But this time, the game is different. The riddles aren’t just clues—they’re personal. Someone knows Hannah. Someone knows her secrets.

As the investigation twists through hidden letters, corrupted memories, and figures from Hannah’s past who were thought long gone, she and Rafael must stay ahead of a killer who’s not just reliving history... but rewriting it. Every discovery points to something darker buried beneath the town’s polished facade—an old betrayal, a secret pact, and the revival of a legacy drenched in blood.

And what the killer wants now… is closure. “What begins with love... ends with silence.”

The Cast

Meet the Characters

01

Hannah Walker

A student majoring in criminal justice who has always aspired to be like Sherlock Holmes. When her writing professor is murdered, she wants to solve the case. But when faculty members are murdered one by one, it is up to Hannah and her godfather Rafael to solve the case before they become victims in Deadly College Murders.

02

Rafael Walker-Smith

Rafael is a former cop and Hannah’s godfather. Devoted to his goddaughter, he is the one who will be there for Hannah when everyone else betrays her. As she suffers a personal tragedy, Rafael will do his best to ensure her safety, going as far as placing her in witness protection. As the bodies drop and her dreams continue to disturb her, Rafael will be the one to help her solve the case before he becomes a victim himself.

03

Professor Gonzalez

Meet Professor Gonzalez, a writing professor whom Hannah despises. When we meet him, we discover he has harassed her and threatened her not to go to the campus police. He is married, but he has disturbing secrets that continue to unfold even after his death. After he is murdered, Hannah is accused of his death, and it will be up to her to bring him justice.

04

Daniel Jimenez

Daniel is Hannah’s best friend. She has been in love with him for several years, but he doesn’t feel the same. When Hannah is accused, it will be up to him and Rafael to uncover the mystery. He is majoring in Astronomy. Will he and Hannah end up together once the mystery is solved?

05

Bree Garcia

Introduced in Deadly Vows: Echoes of College Crimes, Bree is Daniel’s new girlfriend. When Hannah comes back to town, Bree is eager to meet her. She aspires to be like Hannah, but Hannah warns her that what she once aspired to cost her the one thing she never wanted to lose: her freedom. Bree will be the one who helps Hannah on her journey when she gets into a serious accident.

06

Sofia Johnson

Sofia is Hannah’s best friend, a lawyer from New York who moved to be with Hannah after getting a job offer. When Hannah is accused of the Deadly College Murders, Sofia starts showing her dark side. Will she betray Hannah?
