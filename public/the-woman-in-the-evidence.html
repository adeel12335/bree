The Woman in the Evidence is the second book in the Astrid Fitzgerald Mystery Series. A fractured mirror, a woman’s reflection, and evidence that suggests someone is hiding the truth.

What looks settled in the glass does not match the story around it. The proof starts to split, and Astrid has to decide which reflection is the one she can trust.
