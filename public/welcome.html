A Note from Bree

Welcome!

Hi everyone! My name is Bree Wilson, and I am a renowned murder mystery author. My writing journey began in 2019, and since then I’ve devoted myself to crafting suspenseful, character-driven tales that keep readers guessing. You can find more information about who I am and my work on my About page.

I have been writing the Hannah Walker Mystery Series, beginning with Deadly College Murders and continuing with Deadly Vows: Echoes of College Crimes, and Paris Noir: Deadly Obsession. The last two novels earned honors at the International Impact Book Awards in 2025 and 2026, respectively,. I have been nominated for Author of the Year in the true crime category. If you would like to contact me, please feel free to complete the contact form. You can also email me with any questions, and I invite you to explore and follow my social media channels.
