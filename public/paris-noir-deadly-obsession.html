Hannah Walker Mystery · Book 3

Paris NoirDeadly Obsession

Every Clue Leads to Paris. Everybody Is a Message. And He’s Always One Step Ahead.

2025 International Impact Book Awards winner — Mystery/Thriller: Psychological Thrillers

Shop Now

Amazon

The bells are ringing in Paris, but for Hannah, they sound like a funeral knell. A wedding day meant for celebration dissolves into a nightmare of blood and twisted metal when a hooded driver strikes down her beloved godfather, Rafael, in a calculated act of violence.

In Paris Noir: Deadly Obsession, the altar becomes a place of shadows rather than light. While Rafael desperately tries to bury the sins of a past that has finally caught up to him, Hannah is consumed by haunting, prophetic dreams—visceral visions of a wedding ceremony that is always cut short, leaving her silenced before she can ever utter the words “I do.”

They are no longer just victims; they are pieces in a sadistic game of cat and mouse. A hooded figure stalks their every move, leaving behind a trail of cryptic riddles that pulse with a mocking, silent laughter. From the rain-slicked streets of Paris to the neon-lit deceptions of Las Vegas, Hannah must navigate a world where nothing is as it seems.

With the help of an enigmatic illusionist who teaches her to see through the veil of lies, Hannah races to unmask the mastermind before the final riddle is solved. In this deadly obsession, the clues are written in malice, and the truth is buried deep in the shadows of a past Rafael never wanted her to find.

Will Hannah finally get to say her vows, or will the hooded figure ensure her silence is permanent? The answers lie in Paris Noir: Deadly Obsession.

A “Deadly Riddle” Teaser

“A vow is a promise, a ring is a chain,
One leads to pleasure, the other to pain.
You wait for the ‘I do’ at the end of the aisle,
But I’ll give you ‘I die’ with a hooded smile.”
