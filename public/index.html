About Bree T. Wilson

A Passion for
Unraveling Truths

Bree T. Wilson is a four-time International Impact Book Awards Distinguished Author winner. Wilson began her writing journey in 2019 while studying Administration of Justice. Fueled by a deep fascination with mysteries and moral complexities, she found inspiration in her sharp-minded professor, R.G., whose quiet strength and intellect later became the blueprint for Rafael, a central figure in her debut novel.

Her passion for unraveling truths—both hidden and haunting—guides her pursuit of modern mystery, echoing her dream of becoming a contemporary Sherlock Holmes.
