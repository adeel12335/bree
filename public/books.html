Hannah Walker Mystery Series

Hannah Walker Mystery · Book 1

Deadly College Murders

Trust no one. Decode everything. Run, if you still can.

Hannah Walker didn’t come to college to fear for her life—she came to chase a dream: solving the kinds of mysteries only the brilliant minds conquer. Majoring in Administration of Justice, she always idolized Sherlock Holmes. But when her disturbingly eccentric writing professor is found gruesomely murdered, her dream curdles into a nightmare.

Deadly VowsEchoes of College Crimes

“What begins with love... ends with silence.”

Hannah Walker swore she left the darkness of college behind. But when she returns to her hometown for her best friend Connie’s wedding, an eerie sense of déjà vu creeps in. Whispers from the past echo louder, and the nightmare begins anew.

Hannah Walker Mystery · Book 3

Paris NoirDeadly Obsession

Every Clue Leads to Paris. Everybody Is a Message. And He’s Always One Step Ahead.

The bells are ringing in Paris, but for Hannah, they sound like a funeral knell. A wedding day meant for celebration dissolves into a nightmare of blood and twisted metal when a hooded driver strikes down her beloved godfather, Rafael, in a calculated act of violence.
