International Impact Book Awards

Awards

A four-time International Impact Book Awards Distinguished Author winner.

InternationalImpactBook Awards4-TimeAward Winner

Bree T. Wilson is a four-time International Impact Book Awards Distinguished Author winner. Her third book, Paris Noir: Deadly Obsession, won at the International Impact Book Awards in 2025. Her second book, Deadly Vows: Echoes of College Crimes, won in April 2026, making Wilson a two-time IIBA winner.

Her first book, Deadly College Murders, and her upcoming fourth book, The Thirteenth Riddle: A Poisoned Silence, also won at the IIBA in August 2026, making Wilson a four-time award-winning author. She is up for Author of the Year at the International Impact Book Awards in the True Crime category for Paris Noir: Deadly Obsession.
