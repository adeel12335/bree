Paris Noir: Deadly Obsession Out Now!

By Bree T. Wilson · Paris Noir: Deadly Obsession

I’m excited to share that my third book, Paris Noir: Deadly Obsession, is now available on Amazon! The latest installment in the Hannah Walker Mystery Series delves into dark themes of revenge, haunting premonitions, desire, and an obsession that goes beyond imagination. At the heart of the story is Rafael, whose past as a cop comes back to haunt him when a man named Donovan seeks revenge. Rafael soon realizes Hannah, his beloved goddaughter, is in grave danger—not only because Donovan is obsessed with him, but also because he’s determined to hurt her. While Hannah worries for her godfather, she’s unaware of the peril she faces until she uncovers something she never thought possible. In this noir mystery thriller, nothing and no one are as they seem, and the outcome may be deadly in demise. Will Hannah be able to marry her greatest love—Jonathan? Or will her dreams of not making it down the aisle come true? The answer lie in the conclusion of the Hannah Walker Mystery Series in Paris Noir: Deadly Obsession
