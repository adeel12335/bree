Hannah and Jonathan’s love story is full of passion and challenges, but will they make it through and finally say “I do?”

By Bree T. Wilson · Deadly Vows: Echoes of College Crimes

Hannah meets Jonathan in Deadly Vows: Echoes of College Crimes. Jonathan is a private investigator, and from the very moment they meet, Hannah feels an unexpected spark between them. Still, Hannah—true to herself—wants to keep her distance and avoid getting emotionally involved with him.

After she returns to the States for Connie’s wedding, Hannah encounters a handsome stranger named Jonathan. She is understandably wary, especially given everything that happened to her three years earlier. When the hooded figure coerces a victim into tampering with Hannah’s brakes, she is caught in a horrific and massive car crash. As she slowly regains consciousness, she meets this stranger again, and their initial acquaintance deepens: what begins as a tentative friendship gradually blossoms into more tender feelings. Tragically, when Hannah travels to New Orleans to investigate Sofia’s past, she once again crosses paths with the would-be killer, who brutally assaults her and leaves her in a coma. When Hannah wakes with Rafael at her bedside, he is devastated to learn that his dearly loved goddaughter has lost her memory.

After Jonathan learns of this, he understands he must be patient with her and give her time. He’s quietly confident that, with care and the unfolding truth, she will gradually regain her memory. As the weeks go by and the investigation into Connie’s death and the details of Hannah’s past come to light, Hannah slowly begins to feel something for Jonathan again, and his hope grows that her memories will return. When Hannah explodes at Rafael, blaming him for their near-death experience and accusing him of causing the danger they faced, Jonathan moves to reach her emotionally. He keeps speaking gently and persistently, trying to break through the fog that clouds her mind. Eventually his steady words penetrate, and as Hannah reads Rafael’s letter, a sudden clarity returns — she remembers who he is. Overcome, she confesses to Jonathan that she feels tremendous guilt for what she did, convinced she had acted wrongly; Jonathan comforts her, explaining that her reaction was natural given that she had no recollection of him at the time.

After the reveal, Hannah awakens and, blinking in confusion, asks Rafael and Jonathan who they are. Worried she might be suffering from amnesia again, she masks her fear with a nervous grin and jokes that her sense of humor has returned. As Hannah and the others make their way back to Paris, Jonathan quietly plans a romantic proposal at the foot of the Eiffel Tower, and there she softly answers, “Yes.”

In Paris Noir: Deadly Obsession, the couple is planning their wedding in Paris to begin their new life together. After Rafael is run down by a mysterious hooded figure, Hannah and the others return to the United States to investigate Rafael’s past and uncover how it is tied to Hannah’s own history.

Along the way, Hannah discovers she is pregnant and then suffers devastating rejection from Jonathan. Concerned that Jonathan may be under pressure or being blackmailed by someone, Rafael quietly launches his own investigation to uncover the truth. As they prepare to leave the States, Hannah encounters Tatiana, an illusionist from Las Vegas, and kindly invites her to the wedding. As Hannah prepares to say “I do,” she is once again disturbed by recurring dreams in which she never gets to speak those two words. Will the haunting visions Hannah has been having come true? Or will she and Jonathan find their way to the happy ending they both crave? The answer lies in Paris Noir: Deadly Obsession.
