The Thirteenth Riddle A Poisoned Silence… A Hannah Walker Mystery Series.

By Bree T. Wilson · The Thirteenth Riddle: A Poisoned Silence

The Thirteenth Riddle: A Poisoned Silence… A Hannah Walker Mystery Series follows the relentless pursuits of Detective Hannah Walker. Hannah, a woman once haunted by killer riddles, deadly vows, and bloodied dresses, is now a cold-hearted, no-nonsense detective driven by a quiet, steely resolve.

After the events of Paris Noir, Hannah has no time for games or for pretending to be some kind of amateur Sherlock Holmes. Her relationship with Rafael has grown cold over the past couple of years, leaving an undercurrent of unease and a steady sense of distrust in everything he says and does. Still, despite the distance and the doubts, she cannot wholly let go—there remains a quiet, complicated care for him beneath the surface.

On top of that, Hannah is now a new mom to a baby girl named Shayla. Despite her tiny age, Shayla seems wise beyond her years and adores her mother, even though Hannah will soon head off to Europe in search of the antidote needed to save Rafael’s life.

What will make The Thirteenth Riddle stand out from the other books is the deepening bond between Hannah and Rafa — how their trust, tensions, and shared history are tested and transformed. How far will Hannah be willing to go to solve the case and save her godfather’s life?

What has happened with Rafa over the last couple of years after the events of Paris Noir? Rafael still works as a professor but this time from home, maintaining his courses and research remotely. He chose not to relocate, not wanting to leave Hannah alone, especially after the tense showdown in Paris all those years ago. The experience left both of them more cautious, so Rafa has focused on creating a stable, quieter life—balancing his academic responsibilities with the small, steady routines that help them both feel safer.

After learning that Gonzalez’s ex has been found murdered, Rafael becomes single-minded about Hannah’s safety and keeping her focused, but he can’t help noticing unsettling changes in his beloved goddaughter and realizes she is becoming a cold‑hearted detective. When he gently tries to reason with Hannah about the direction she’s taking, their conversation escalates into a tense argument — and the confrontation takes a darker turn when Rafael is abducted.

After she learns her beloved godfather has been kidnapped, Hannah goes rogue and finds a way to rescue him, navigating danger and deception with a fierce, determined clarity she hadn’t known she possessed. Once she manages to get him free, the adrenaline finally drains away and she collapses into his arms, sobbing as she apologizes for the coldness and distance she’s shown him. She confesses that the horrific events in Paris all those years ago left her deeply traumatized, and that fear of losing him has made her push him away instead of holding on. She cries for hours in his embrace, letting out the pain she’s carried alone, and eventually, exhausted, falls asleep clinging to him. The next day Rafael is rushed to the hospital, and Hannah faces a gut‑wrenching truth: he has been poisoned. The only hope for an antidote lies somewhere in Lucerne, at a place called Die Klinik den Geist Zu Halen—the clinic to heal the mind—or, in some cases, to erase minds for good.

The love she feels for her godfather drives Hannah to do the unthinkable: she ingests the very toxin that is already coursing through his veins. With both of them poisoned, their only hope rests with a doctor named Stu, whose tangled past with Gonzalez’s ex-wife holds the crucial clue to an antidote that could save Hannah and Rafael. As the poison takes hold, Hannah is tormented by vivid dreams and haunting visions: a long hallway of mirrors reflecting fractured, distorted versions of herself; a ghostly train roaring through a thick, impenetrable fog with faceless passengers staring blankly; and a small tea table where Christina’s trembling hand reveals a hidden key beneath a shattered cup. Each dream arrives like a fragment of a puzzle — part clue, part warning — pushing Hannah closer to the truth and the race for a cure.

Will Hannah be able to save Rafael? Will she find the truth she is looking for? Or will The Thirteenth Riddle be her last? The answers lie in: The Thirteenth Riddle: A Poisoned Silence
