Paris Noir: Deadly Obsession wins award

By Bree T. Wilson · Paris Noir: Deadly Obsession

I am absolutely thrilled to announce that my third book, Paris Noir: Deadly Obsession, has won an award at the prestigious International Impact Book Awards! This recognition is such an incredible honor and represents a major milestone in my writing career. I feel both honored and humbled that my third installment in the Hannah Walker Mystery Series has received such acclaim. When I first began writing the very first book, Deadly College Murders, I never imagined it would grow into a full-fledged series that resonates with readers. Through the story of Hannah and her godfather Rafael, readers will be fully immersed from the very moment they open the first book. While I won't reveal how the third installment concludes, I can say that I was genuinely excited about how I crafted the ending—a part of the writing process that I struggled with greatly. When I started writing the second one, I knew it had to be significantly better than the first one, and I realized it was essential to add several unexpected twists and turns to keep readers engaged. The third one focuses entirely on Rafael, exploring his complex past and detailing how his bond with his goddaughter grows deeper and more meaningful over time.
